About

A respectful, structured path toward recovery

Vyakti Nirman Nasha Mukti Center in Nagpur provides non-medical recovery and lifestyle support for people working to move away from substance dependence. Each enquiry begins with a private conversation so families can understand whether our programs are suitable.

Our programs focus on healthy routines, self-discipline, emotional well-being and ongoing support. We communicate clearly with families about the support we provide and when a qualified medical professional should be consulted.
